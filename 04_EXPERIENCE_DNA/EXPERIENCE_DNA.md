# CAR SERVICE 06 — EXPERIENCE DNA

## Central idea

**TRANSFORMATION IS THE STORY.**

The website should not tell the visitor that the service is premium through adjectives alone.

It should make the visitor see:

dirty / ordinary → intervention → transformation → finish → desire.

## Emotional progression

```text
CURIOSITY
   ↓
TENSION
   ↓
TRANSFORMATION
   ↓
SATISFACTION
   ↓
DESIRE
   ↓
ACTION
```

## Cinematic grammar

### Arrival

Do not immediately explain everything.

Establish:

- darkness
- environment
- silhouette
- sound/visual atmosphere where appropriate
- anticipation

### Water / foam

Water should behave as a transition material.

Avoid particle wallpaper.

### Transformation

The supplied before/after video should be treated as evidence of the real transformation.

Potential treatments:

- masked reveal
- controlled scrub
- spatial integration
- split transformation
- material transition

Choose based on the actual video's composition.

### Finish

Focus on:

- reflections
- water beads
- paint
- glass
- wheels
- controlled highlights

Only use effects supported by real visual assets or physically coherent 3D.

### Interior

If usable vehicle assets exist, make the camera discover the cabin rather than presenting an inventory.

### Rental

Rental should feel like mobility.

Avoid leading with three pricing cards.

Possible narrative:

darkness → vehicle presence → vehicle reveal → practical offer → action.

## Typography

Typography is part of the spatial composition.

Use restrained hierarchy.

Avoid huge marketing headlines occupying the entire screen without a compositional reason.

## Color

Do not automatically combine yellow/gold and red at full intensity.

Establish a primary visual system and use the second accent only where it has narrative/commercial purpose.

## Interaction

Micro-interactions must communicate:

- progress
- precision
- responsiveness
- materiality

Not decoration.

## AI-smell rejection

The experience fails this test if a viewer can describe it as:

“dark background + luxury font + giant headline + car + glow + cards.”

That combination is prohibited as a default solution.
