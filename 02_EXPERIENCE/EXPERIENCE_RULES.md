# CAR SERVICE 06 — EXPERIENCE RULES

## Principle

The user should feel a transformation, not browse a list of services.

Narrative:
EMOTION → CURIOSITY → TRANSFORMATION → DESIRE → ACTION

## Visual identity

Source material points toward:
- black / deep dark base
- yellow/gold premium cleaning language
- white typography
- water, foam, gloss, reflections
- a separate red accent appears in the rental flyer

Do not automatically merge every flyer color into one palette. Resolve the visual system after inspecting the complete asset corpus.

## Motion

Motion must communicate:
- water
- pressure
- cleaning
- reveal
- shine
- arrival
- mobility

Every major movement needs a narrative reason.

## Premium rule

If an effect does not improve:
- depth
- transformation
- comprehension
- atmosphere
- navigation

remove it.
